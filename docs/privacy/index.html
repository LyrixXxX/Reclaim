Reclaim is a personal habits, health, nutrition, CPAP, weight, and activity tracking app. It does not require a Reclaim account. Records you enter are stored on your device unless you choose a feature that sends information elsewhere.

Information stored on your device

Reclaim stores the trackers, readings, meals, photos, step history, GPS routes, preferences, and reports you choose to create. The app-lock PIN verifier is kept in secure device storage and excluded from portable backups. You can edit or delete records in the app, export a backup, or remove local data through Android settings. Backups remain wherever you save or share them.

Device permissions

With your permission, Reclaim can use the activity sensor for step counts; location for an active route and map positioning; camera or selected photos for food labels, CPAP screens, or lab reports; and notifications for active tracking and reminders. Background location is used only while a route you started is recording. You can turn off features or revoke permissions in Android settings. [VERIFY AGAINST THE FINAL BUILD.]

Optional network features

When you view or download a map, Reclaim requests map tiles from Stadia Maps. Those requests can reveal the approximate area viewed and your network address to the map provider. When you explicitly submit a food description, label image, or laboratory-report text through a configured Private AI relay, that content is sent to the relay and its AI processing provider. Reclaim does not send local health records merely because they are stored in the app. [IDENTIFY THE RELAY OPERATOR AND RETENTION PRACTICES.]

Advertising and payment

The free version may show a Google-served, non-personalized banner on Home. Google's ad software may process device identifiers, network information, and ad interactions for delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement. Reclaim does not pass tracker names, health readings, meals, steps, or routes as targeting information. Where required, Reclaim asks for ad consent and offers privacy choices. Google Play handles Remove Ads payments. Reclaim sends a purchase token to its verification service, which checks with Google Play and does not store that token.
