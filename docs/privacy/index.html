Privacy Policy

Reclaim helps you track habits, nutrition, vitamins and supplements, weight, steps, strength workouts, routes, and sleep routines. It does not require a Reclaim account and does not provide medical guidance.

Updated: November 1, 2026

Operator: Reclaim is operated by an individual developer under the developer name Lyrix Studios.

Privacy contact: Reclaim.LyrixStudios@gmail.com

Information you keep on your device

Reclaim stores the information you choose to enter or record on your device: habits and sleep routines, weight records, meals and food photos, vitamin and supplement schedules, stacks and dose history, step totals and walking-time intervals, strength workouts and routines, saved GPS routes, goals, preferences, and reports. If you update from an older full-featured Reclaim version, its medical records remain in local storage and portable backups, but this wellness edition does not display or update them. An app-lock PIN verifier is held in secure device storage and is not included in portable backups. Reclaim does not maintain a cloud account or a server-side database of these tracker records.

You can edit or delete wellness records in the app, edit or archive supplement entries and remove their logs, export a backup, or clear all of Reclaim's local data through Android settings. Older medical records retained from previous versions cannot be edited in this edition. Exported backups remain wherever you choose to save or share them; clearing the app does not remove those copies.

Permissions and location

If you enable phone step tracking, Reclaim uses the phone's activity sensors. Where supported, time-stamped step events create local walking-time intervals for Walk more goals; time spent recording a route is excluded from phone walking minutes. These intervals remain in native device storage and are not included in portable backups. Reclaim no longer connects to Health Connect. If you previously imported scale weigh-ins, those records remain in your local Weight history, retain their source labels, and may be included in a backup you export. You can delete them in Weight. Opening the Routes map uses foreground location to show your position, even before you start recording. If you choose to record a walk, hike, or run, Reclaim saves route points on your device. A route can continue recording while the app is in the background or the phone is locked, until you pause or finish it. Camera or selected-photo access supports food and optional supplement-label logging. Notifications support habit reminders and active tracking. You can turn off features or revoke permissions in Android settings.

Information sent when you choose network features

  • Macro questionnaire: In current builds, the guided macro calculator runs on your device. Its answers are not sent to the AI relay or saved as a profile. Older builds may still offer an AI-assisted macro plan. Only if you request that plan, age, height, current weight, activity level, goal, training focus, loss pace and priority, estimation basis, and any calorie target you enter go to the Cloudflare relay. The relay calculates a maintenance estimate and protein starting point; only the resulting calorie target, source label, protein target, goal, activity, training focus, pace, and priority go onward to OpenAI—not your age, height, weight, or estimation basis.
  • Other AI features: When you choose to request a food analysis, produce-serving suggestion, or strength-routine draft, the content you submit is sent over HTTPS through a Lyrix Studios relay hosted by Cloudflare to OpenAI. Strength planning sends your chosen training days, session length, equipment, experience, muscle focus, and built-in exercise names, but not your workout history, logged weights, or saved routines. Do not include information you do not want processed by these providers. Reclaim does not send your entire tracker history merely because it is on your device. The relay does not intentionally store the submitted content or AI response. It uses network information, including an IP-derived identifier, for abuse limits and verifies eligible Play-installed app requests with Google Play Integrity. The OpenAI request sets store: false, but OpenAI may retain API abuse-monitoring data under its own policy (normally up to 30 days, with exceptions). Manual tracking remains available without AI.
  • Optional supplement-label scanning: If you choose a cropped Supplement Facts photo and request AI assistance, that selected image is sent through the Cloudflare relay to OpenAI to draft a product name and readable label amounts. No supplement is saved until you review and confirm the draft. Reclaim does not send your full supplement history or recommend a dose. You can set up supplements manually without AI.
  • Maps: Viewing maps requests tiles from Stadia Maps. The provider can receive your network address and the map area requested. Saved route records remain on your device unless you export them.
  • Routes weather: While the Routes screen is open, Reclaim may request an hourly forecast from the U.S. National Weather Service using an approximate version of your current coordinates. The service receives those coordinates and network information such as your IP address. Weather is available only where the National Weather Service provides forecasts; Reclaim does not fetch it in the background. Weather requests are separate from recording and saving routes.
  • Backups and sharing: If you export or share a backup or report, the destination you choose receives that file. Lyrix Studios does not receive it automatically.

Advertising and purchases

Current test builds show Google test ads, which earn no revenue. If live ads are enabled in a later version, Reclaim may show Google-served, non-personalized banners on Home, Steps, and Nutrition, plus ads after logging a current meal, saving a route, or saving a new current-day weigh-in. The Google Mobile Ads software may process device or advertising identifiers, network information (which can indicate an approximate location), consent choices, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Reclaim does not pass your tracker names, meals, steps, workouts, or routes as ad-targeting information. Where required, Reclaim requests ad consent before requesting ads and offers privacy choices.

If a Remove Ads purchase is offered, Google Play handles the payment. Reclaim sends its purchase token and app/product identifiers to Lyrix Studios' verification service, which checks the purchase with Google Play. The verification service does not intentionally store the token. Purchase availability depends on the app version and store configuration.

Security, retention, and your choices

Network features use HTTPS. Local records remain until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app; copies you exported are under your control. The AI relay and purchase verifier are designed without a tracker-record database. Service providers may retain network, security, or processing data according to their policies. Reclaim does not sell your health or location records.

For questions about this policy, or to ask Lyrix Studios about information it controls, email Reclaim.LyrixStudios@gmail.com. Because Reclaim has no user account or cloud tracker database, you can review and remove local tracker data directly in the app or Android settings. You must delete any exported copies from the places where you saved them.

Additional information for people in the EU/EEA

Reclaim provides the local tracking features you choose to use. For optional network features, information is processed to respond to your request (such as loading a map or analyzing selected food content), to keep the service secure and limit abuse, or, when enabled and permitted, to deliver ads. The legal basis depends on the processing: providing a service you request, legitimate interests in security and abuse prevention, or consent where requested for optional processing and advertising identifiers. You can stop using an optional feature or change a consent choice where available. This does not undo processing already carried out.

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Email the privacy contact above to ask about data Lyrix Studios controls; we may need information to verify your request. Most tracker records exist only on your phone, so you can view, edit, export, or delete them there. You may also lodge a complaint with the data-protection authority in your EU/EEA country.

Cloudflare, OpenAI, Google, Stadia Maps, and the U.S. National Weather Service may process data outside your country, including outside the EU/EEA, when you use the relevant network feature. Contact us if you need more information about an applicable provider's international-transfer safeguards. Their own privacy notices describe their processing and retention practices.
